# Competitor Research — Indonesian Gen-Z Media Accounts

Reference study of the visual + copy style of Indonesian Instagram media accounts
(Cretivox, Folkative, USS Feed) to inform our own post templates and wording.

> Note on sources: Instagram profile pages can't be scraped directly (login wall),
> so typography specifics below are **observational patterns** (general aesthetic
> genre) rather than exact published font names. Language style + format findings
> are **verified** via peer-reviewed studies (cited inline).

---

## 1. The three accounts at a glance

| Account | Audience | Content focus | Tone |
|---------|----------|---------------|------|
| **@creativox** | Gen Z + millennial (800k+) | Info aktual, hiburan, isu populer, sindiran (satire), zodiak, life | Sarkastik, relatable, bahasa gaul |
| **@folkative** | Gen Z, millennials | Karya anak muda: seni, musik, film, desain, budaya, brand lokal | Kolokial, santai, ekspresif |
| **@ussfeed** | Gen Z | Pop culture + tech/gadget, series "Just So You Know" | Ringkas, informatif, netral-ramah |

**Shared positioning (verified, The Conversation / research on 100 Gen-Z Jabodetabek):**
- Win on **hiburan (78–79%)** and **kenyamanan (72–74%)** satisfaction vs mainstream news.
- Format: "tipografi singkat, infografis, atau video kurang dari satu menit."
- Content is **feed-native** — consumed without leaving the app.
- Assume the reader **already has context**; konten dibuat singkat, tidak detail.
- Topics follow what's trending on TikTok / X / other platforms.

## 2. Language & wording style (verified)

**Folkative caption study** (20 captions, June–July 2025, *Argo Puro* journal):
- **Colloquial: 10** · Informal: 7 · Formal: 3.
- Emojis used as tone markers: 🔥 fire, 🫶 heart-hands, 👏 clapping hands.

**Folkative branding study** (*Prologia* 2024):
- "bahasa yang mudah dimengerti", "penyampaian informasi singkat, padat, dan jelas."

**Cretivox** (UMN + media akademik journals):
- "informasi tercepat, kata-kata sindiran, gaya bahasa modern, visual atraktif."
- Captions are "komunikatif" and **credit the source**.
- Copy-based content built from: **headline + subline + caption** in a signature tone.

### Practical copy recipe (what to imitate)

**Caption structure:**
```
[HOOK — 1 kalimat provokatif/relatable, sering berupa pertanyaan]

[ISI — 1–3 kalimat pendek, dipisah line break, bukan paragraf]

[CTA — "menurut kalian gimana?", "setuju nggak?", "spill di kolom komentar"]

[Sumber: nama outlet]

[Hashtags]
```

**Tone rules:**
- Bahasa gaul / informal, tapi tetap jelas dan tidak lebay.
- Kalimat pendek. Satu slide = satu ide = satu kalimat.
- Emoji dipakai sebagai pemisah/bullet, bukan dekorasi berlebihan.
- Angka/data ditonjolkan ("3 alasan", "Rp 1,2 T", "85%").

## 3. Post format (verified + observational)

**Carousel (format paling umum di ketiga akun):**
- Slide 1 = **hook besar** (scroll-stopper, bold, 3–7 kata).
- Slide 2–5 = **satu poin per slide**, teks minimal, banyak whitespace.
- Slide terakhir = **CTA + handle/logo**.

**Single image / infografis:**
- Judul tebal + 1 fakta kunci + sumber kecil di pojok.

**Reels:**
- < 60 detik, hook di 3 detik pertama, text overlay, audio trending.

**Common thread:** strong first slide, phone-first readability, one idea per slide.

## 4. Typography & visual identity (observational genre)

All three share a "clean geometric sans, high contrast, color block" aesthetic.
Exact fonts are custom/branded; below are free equivalents that match the genre.

### Recommended font stack for our pipeline

| Role | Free font | Why |
|------|-----------|-----|
| **Headline/display** | **Archivo Black** / **Anton** / **Clash Display** | Bold, condensed, punchy — matches "big hook" style |
| **Body** | **Plus Jakarta Sans** | Designed for Jakarta/Indonesia (Tokotype); clean, warm, very on-brand |
| **Alt body** | **Inter** / **Manrope** | Neutral, readable at small sizes |
| **Accent (data/angka)** | **Space Grotesk** / **JetBrains Mono** | Techy feel for stats, handles, hashtags |

**Rules:**
- Max **2–3 typefaces** per post (headline + body + optional mono accent).
- Hierarchy: headline HUGE (≥72px at 1080×1350), body 36–48px.
- High contrast: solid color block background + white/black text. Avoid busy photos behind text.
- 2–3 signature colors per account; use one accent per slide.

### Signature color personalities (observational)
- **Cretivox** — bold, high-saturation, gradient/playful (meme-adjacent but polished).
- **Folkative** — editorial primary colors (red/yellow/blue) + lots of black/white.
- **USS Feed** — clean minimal "news card", white/black + single accent.

### IG technical specs
- Carousel/single: **1080×1350 (4:5)** or 1080×1080.
- Reels: **1080×1920 (9:16)**, safe margins for UI overlay (keep text in center ~1080×1420).
- Slide 1 text must be readable in a small grid thumbnail.

## 4b. Jakarta Keras (added research)

**@jakarta.keras** — 7M+ audience across platforms, founded 2016. Youth culture,
lifestyle, communities. One of Indonesia's biggest cultural voices.

Verified findings (studies):
- **Headline-driven**: an SFL study of 50 headlines found heavy use of material/
  relational processes — direct, action-based statements, not abstract news-speak.
- Audience slang-heavy (sociolinguistic study of comment sections).

Observational design genre:
- **Sticker/badge aesthetic**: solid-color rounded pills as labels, strong contrast,
  high-impact condensed/black headlines, often UPPERCASE.
- Gritty streetwear/pop energy vs Folkative's softer editorial primary colors.
- Post = few slides, big cover, punchy text; detail goes in the caption.

### Updated design implications for our templates
1. **Fewer slides**: cover (image + hook overlay) + up to 3 point slides + CTA = max 5.
   The full explanation lives in the caption, not the carousel.
2. **Cover = real image**: article photo full-bleed + dark gradient + white hook text
   + sticker badge (category pill) — like a mini poster.
3. **Short hooks (≤5 words) dipakai UPPERCASE** (Jakarta Keras sticker style); longer
   hooks stay sentence case for readability.
4. Keep Archivo Black / Plus Jakarta Sans Bold / Space Grotesk; badge text color
   auto-contrasts against the accent.

## 6. Riset lanjutan: struktur berita & tipografi

### Struktur paragraf (dari pedoman jurnalistik)

Berita klasik Indonesia (PWI, Kompas, materi jurnalistik dasar) pakai struktur:
- **Teras berita (lead)**: paragraf pertama, fakta terpenting, 2–3 kalimat,
  menganut 5W+1H — menarik perhatian pembaca.
- **Tubuh berita (body)**: menguraikan lead dengan detail, angka, konteks.
- **Ekor**: konteks tambahan.

Diterapkan ke slide isi kita:
- Slide 1 (paragraf lead) = apa yang terjadi, fakta terpenting.
- Slide 2 (paragraf body) = detail, angka, dampak buat pembaca.

### Tipografi

- **Plus Jakarta Sans** terbukti relevan: studi tipografi city branding Jakarta
  menjadikannya studi kasus utama (Jurnal Riset Multidisiplin Edukasi).
- Tren tipografi 2025: "sky-high sans serifs" (display sans besar-besar) —
  konsisten dengan pilihan Archivo Black untuk hook/cover.
- Pedoman dasar Instagram typography: 2–3 typeface maksimal, hierarki jelas,
  phone-first readability (font besar, kontras tinggi).

### Bahasa (update)

- Partikel emotif (kan, nih, tuh, lho, kok, deh, sih, dong) adalah penanda
  utama bahasa gaul Jakarta yang natural (Bahasakita, kajian UNAIR/Badan Bahasa).
- Slang + code-switching Inggris (Jaksel) umum dipakai akun media Gen Z;
  orthographic play (nggak/gak, udah, bikin) adalah norma.

## 7. What this means for our pipeline (M3 templates)

1. **Struktur slide**: `01-cover` (foto + hook), lalu paragraf lead dan paragraf body
   masing-masing satu slide — maksimal 2 slide isi, teks di-center vertikal
   dengan padding kiri-kanan-atas-bawah seimbang.
2. **One reel template** (M7): hook overlay (first 3s) + 3 beats + CTA, Plus Jakarta Sans.
3. **Caption generator** must output the structure in §2 (hook, isi, CTA, sumber, hashtags) in Bahasa Indonesia, colloquial tone.
4. **Design system config** (fonts, colors, sizes) lives in `src/render/theme.ts` so templates are swappable.

## 8. Tren tipografi 2026

Riset tren terkini (PRINT Type Report, Creative Bloq, Fontfabric, Canva Design
Trends Report 2026):

- **"Imperfect by Design"** (Canva 2026): pergeseran dari desain sempurna ke
  visual yang manusiawi, personal, autentik.
- **Ekspresif kembali**: setelah era neo-grotesk bersih (Inter/Geist), pendulum
  balik ke ekspresi — serif lantang, display weight ekstrem.
- **Prediksi Indonesia**: warna neon, palet earth-tone hangat, tipografi besar,
  bentuk geometrik eksperimental.
- **Mixing**: display ekspresif + body sans tenang adalah pairing yang aman.

Implikasi untuk desain kita (sudah/akan diterapkan):
- Palet cream earth-tone hangat ✅ (sudah on-trend).
- Archivo Black sebagai display besar ✅ (sesuai "tipografi besar").
- Opsi ke depan: aksen serif untuk kalimat pembuka, atau drop-cap di slide lead.
